B. GEOMETRIC / MATHEMATICAL INTERPRETATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[V]  Foot, R.
     "A note on Koide's lepton mass relation"
     arXiv:hep-ph/9402242 (1994)
     bibkey: Foot1994
     Comment: Establishes Q as cos^2 of angle between
              (sqrt(m_e), sqrt(m_mu), sqrt(m_tau)) and unit diagonal.

[V]  Rivero, A. and Gsponer, A.
     "The strange formula of Dr. Koide"
     arXiv:hep-ph/0505220 (2005)
     bibkey: RiveroGsponer2005
     Comment: Historical and bibliographical review. Notes Q = 2/3 is
              the midpoint of [1/3, 1]. Widely cited entry point.

[V]  Kocik, J.
     "The Koide Lepton Mass Formula and Geometry of Circles"
     arXiv:1201.2067 [physics.gen-ph] (2012)
     bibkey: Kocik2012
     Comment: *** DIRECTLY RELEVANT TO THE SODDY PAPER. ***
              "A remarkable formal similarity between Koide's lepton
              mass formula and a generalized Descartes circle formula
              is reported." Generalizes Descartes to intersecting
              circles at a common angle; shows Koide Q = 2/3 emerges
              when the fourth curvature is set to zero. The Brilliant
              (2026) Soddy paper takes the complementary step of
              computing the non-trivial fourth curvature from the
              ordinary (mutually tangent) Descartes theorem.
              VERIFIED by author as of 2026-04-19.

[S]  Brannen, C.
     "The lepton masses" (brannenworks.com, 2006)
     Comment: Non-peer-reviewed but widely cited. Pattern extension
              to neutrinos via signed sqrt(m).

[V]  Rivero, A.
     "Koide formula: beyond charged leptons" (the waterfall in the
     quark sector)
     Presentation / ResearchGate posting, compiled bibliography of
     Koide-related work. Useful entry point.

C. EXTENSIONS TO QUARKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[V]  Rivero, A.
     "A new Koide tuple: strange-charm-bottom"
     arXiv:1111.7232 [hep-ph] (2011)
     bibkey: Rivero2011
     Comment: *** THE "KOIDE WATERFALL" PAPER. ***
              Shows that (s, c, b) with -sqrt(m_s) satisfies Q = 2/3,
              continuing a (c, b, t) tuple of Rodejohann-Zhang. Notes
              the quasi-orthogonality to the original lepton tuple.
              The chained Koide construction predicts the top mass
              from the lepton sector.

[V]  Rodejohann, W. and Zhang, H.
     "Extension of an empirical charged lepton mass relation to the
     neutrino sector"
     Physics Letters B 698, 152 (2011)
     arXiv:1101.5525
     DOI: 10.1016/j.physletb.2011.03.007
     bibkey: RZ2011 / RodejohannZhang2011
     Comment: Surveys multiple quark/neutrino triples. Introduces
              deviation parameters. First to identify (c, b, t) tuple.

[V]  Cao, F. G.
     "Neutrino masses from lepton and quark mass relations and neutrino
     oscillations"
     Physical Review D 85, 113003 (2012)
     DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevD.85.113003
     bibkey: Cao2012
     Comment: Systematic application to both quark and neutrino sectors.
              Source of the (c, b, t)-at-self-scales claim tested
              in the look-elsewhere paper.

[V]  Kartavtsev, A.
     "A remark on the Koide relation for quarks"
     arXiv:1111.0480 [hep-ph] (2011)
     Comment: Proposes extensions to the quark sector. Note: this is
              a DIFFERENT paper from Rivero arXiv:1111.7232 despite
              both being hep-ph from November 2011.

[V]  Li, N. and Ma, B.-Q. and collaborators
     "Explorations of two empirical formulas for fermion masses"
     European Physical Journal C 76, 167 (2016)
     arXiv:1601.00805
     DOI: 10.1140/epjc/s10052-016-3990-3
     Comment: RG-running analysis of Kartavtsev's extensions across
              scales 1 GeV - 10^12 GeV. Important validation of the
              scale-dependence structure of Koide-type quark relations.

[V]  Krolikowski, W.
     Representative papers:
       - "A universal shape of empirical mass formula for all leptons
          and quarks" arXiv:hep-ph/0602018 (2006)
       - "Predictive empirical mass formula for up and down quarks of
          three generations" arXiv:1011.1120 (2010)
       - "How the tau-lepton mass can be understood"
         arXiv:1009.2388 (2010)
       - Koide-type extension to all six quarks arXiv:1201.1251 (2012)
     bibkey in Brilliant Soddy paper: Krolikowski2014
     Comment: Krolikowski has produced a sequence of empirical mass
              formulas for both leptons and quarks. The precise paper
              behind the "Krolikowski2014" bibkey is not yet
              identified; possibly refers to a 2014 update or to the
              2012 quark extension.  Any of the above are legitimate
              representative further reading.

[V]  Xing, Z.-Z. and Zhang, H.
     "On the Koide-like relations for the running masses of charged
     leptons, neutrinos, and quarks"
     Physics Letters B 635, 107 (2006)
     arXiv:hep-ph/0602134
     Comment: Scheme/scale dependence for running masses.

[V]  Fusaoka, H. and Koide, Y.
     Physical Review D 57, 3986 (1998)
     Comment: Running-mass analysis (older).

[V]  Xing, Z.-Z., Zhang, H., Zhou, S.
     Physical Review D 77, 113016 (2008); Phys. Rev. D 86, 013013 (2012)
     Comment: Running-quark-mass updates.

F. LOOK-ELSEWHERE / STATISTICAL ANALYSES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[V]  Brilliant, A. M.
     "A Constrained-Search Test of the Lepton-Scale Hypothesis on the
     (c, b, t) Heavy Koide Combination"
     Preprints.org (2026), DOI: 10.20944/preprints202604.0693.v1
     bibkey: Brilliant2026cbt
     Comment: The companion look-elsewhere paper. Tests (c, b, t) at
              mu = m_tau against a Donoghue log-uniform random-spectrum
              null. Gets p = 0.002 for the pre-specified claim under
              full four-loop CRunDec; p approx 0.5 for unconstrained
              search space. This is the v3 MC work that we extend.

[V]  Brilliant, A. M.
     "Soddy Curvature of the Koide Lepton Triple and the Strange-Quark
     Mass"
     Preprints.org (2026)
     bibkey: Brilliant2026strange
     Comment: The paper you sent. Koide-conditioned null MC with 4-prior
              sensitivity. p_A1 = 0.340% on 10,000 Koide-valid triples
              under primary log-uniform prior (95% CI: [0.236, 0.475]).

HOW TO CONTRIBUTE (for GitHub once uploaded)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

When adding a new entry:
1. Provide full bibliographic citation (authors, title, journal, vol,
   page, year) AND arXiv number AND (ideally) DOI.
2. Mark verification status with [V] / [S] / [R] / [?].
3. Write a one-paragraph "Comment:" explaining what the paper
   contributes to the Koide literature.
4. Place under the appropriate section.

When two sources give inconsistent citation details, prefer in order:
(1) DOI, (2) arXiv abstract, (3) INSPIRE-HEP, (4) journal publisher.
Wikipedia and HandWiki are tertiary.
